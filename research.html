Research

How messages move people

My projects ask what makes a message believable, relevant, or worth acting on for a particular audience. Most of them are set in Bangladesh, where I have done fieldwork with smokers, rural social media users, and coastal journalists.

Methods

Experiments

Between-subjects message tests of framing, numeric risk, and source effects.

Surveys

Health belief and intention measures with smokers and social media users.

Content analysis

News coverage, campaign videos, and platform content in Bengali and English.

Interviews

In-depth interviews with journalists and rural audiences, coded in NVivo.
