About

I came to research through the newsroom and the fact-check desk.

I am a graduate teaching assistant and MS student in Mass Communications at Oklahoma State University's School of Media and Strategic Communications. Before that I was a graduate research assistant in the Department of Communication, Media and Persuasion at Idaho State University, and I earned my bachelor's and master's in Mass Communication and Journalism at Khulna University, Bangladesh.

I reported for campus and national news portals, worked as a fact-checker at Rumor Scanner during Bangladesh's 2024 political crisis, and co-founded Coastal Voices, a U.S. Department of State–funded media literacy project for coastal journalists. I bring that field experience to experiments, surveys, content analysis, and in-depth interviews.
